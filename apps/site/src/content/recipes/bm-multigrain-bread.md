Bread Maker Multigrain Bread
====

2 lb. loaf for Oster bread maker.

    1 5/8 cup water
    2 tsp salt
    4 2/3 cups '7-grain bread flour'
    1/3 cup brown sugar, packed
    3 tsp active dry yeast

    Cool on wire rack when finished(
        Select 2 lb loaf size and "French"(
            Add to bottom of loaf pan(
                water,
                brown sugar,
                salt
            ),
            Float over top of liquid(
                '7-grain bread flour',
                Create small well in flour and add(
                    active dry yeast
                )
            )
        ),
        Check during inital kneading and add water few drops at a time if needed
    )

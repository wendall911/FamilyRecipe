No-Bake Granola Bars
====

Modified recipe from T's second grade cooking class. Sticky ingredients can be substitued as desired, just need to make sure it is wet enough to stick together. Nuts, nut butter and dried fruit can be substituted with whatever you have available. When fully cooled, cut into bar shapes and place in small snack bags.

    2 cups rolled oats
    1/3 cup flax seeds
    1/2 cup dried cranberries
    1/2 cup raisins
    1/2 cup sunflower seeds
    1 cup nuts, finely chopped
    3/4 cup almond butter
    1/4 cup honey
    1/4 cup agave
    2 tbsp molasess

    Press mixture into prepared baking pan(
        Oil 9x13 baking pan with oil,
        Warm small sauce pan on medium low heat,
        Mix well(
            Mix(
                rolled oats,
                flax seeds,
                dried cranberries,
                raisins,
                sunflower seeds,
                nuts
            ),
            Stir into sauce pan(
                almond butter,
                honey,
                agave,
                molasess
            )
        )
    )

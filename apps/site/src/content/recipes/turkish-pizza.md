Red Pepper and Lamb Lahmacun (Turkish Pizza)
==================================================

Thin, crisp Turkish flatbreads under a spiced red pepper and lamb topping.

    lamb mince = 250g "lamb mince (10% fat)"
    onion = 1 onion, grated
    bell pepper = 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
    4 cloves garlic, crushed
    2 tbsp olive oil
    1 tbsp tomato paste
    1 tbsp ground cumin
    1 tsp red pepper flakes
    1 tsp sumac
    coriander = {1 handful} fresh coriander or parsley, roughly chopped
    [dough](pizza-dough "Dad's pizza dough.")

    Topping := mix(
        lamb mince,
        onion,
        bell pepper,
        garlic,
        olive oil,
        tomato paste,
        ground cumin,
        red pepper flakes,
        sumac,
        coriander,
    )

    bake for 7 minutes (
        preheat pizza stone to 550*f in oven,
        spread thinly (
            place on baking paper (
                roll into thin ovals (
                    divide into {4} (dough)
                )
            ),
            Topping,
        )
    )
